the story

Bazaar café, present day

Tucked into Impala Tower on NH-58, across from the old sugar mill gate, Sky Cafe has become a fixture of Lower Bazaar — the kind of place regulars walk into without checking the menu.

The room runs on contrast: brass trays and hand-painted signage on one side, cheese-corn pizza and hot chocolate fudge on the other. Staff know regulars by their order, and the tables fill up fastest on weekend evenings.
